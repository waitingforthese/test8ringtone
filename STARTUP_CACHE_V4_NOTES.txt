LIFE ALARM — STARTUP CACHE V4

Base: LifeAlarm_STARTUP_FAST_V3_FIXED

Change:
- Last successfully calculated Moon/Sun/Panchang state is cached locally.
- On the next app launch the cached state is rendered immediately instead of
  waiting for the Swiss Ephemeris calculation to finish.
- A fresh calculation still runs in the background and replaces the cache.
- Cache failures are ignored so they can never crash or block the app.
- The cache is display-only; alarm scheduling continues to use fresh calculation.
- Existing location-specific Prahar/Lagna, alarm engine, duplicate control,
  settings and Masa removal are unchanged.
